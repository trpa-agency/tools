Buying or selling in the Tahoe Region

Realtors and New Property Owners

BMP status is disclosed at the point of sale, and the buyer files the form with TRPA within 30 days.

BMP real estate disclosure

Since about 2000, TRPA has required the BMP status of a property within the Tahoe Region to be disclosed during real estate transactions. TRPA currently requires disclosure of a property's BMP status instead of actively enforcing BMP installation at the point of sale.

TRPA Code of Ordinances 60.4.4.C, BMP Retrofit Program disclosure requirements

"Owners of property for sale shall, prior to sale, disclose to a purchaser the property's BMP status on a TRPA approved form. The purchaser of the property shall provide the disclosure form to TRPA within 30 days of sale."

What to do

  1. Check the property's BMP status with the Property BMP Status Search. Open the parcel in the LT Info Parcel Tracker from the result to print a copy of the certificate.
  2. Read the brochure for the property type: A Homeowner's Guide to Best Management Practices in the Lake Tahoe Basin for residential properties, or Clean Water is Everyone's Business for commercial properties.
  3. Complete and submit the disclosure form within 30 days of the sale: the fillable PDF, or print and submit the static version.

BMP Real Estate Disclosure Form (fillable PDF)

Background

Following TRPA Governing Board approval of the BMP Action Plan on Feb. 25, 2015, and a year-long negotiation with stakeholders, an enhanced disclosure form that can be completed and submitted electronically became available. The enhanced form references TRPA's authority under the 1980 Bi-State Compact to impose fines of up to $5,000 per day for violations of TRPA ordinances. TRPA prefers that property owners put those funds into installing and maintaining BMPs, and would notify owners in writing by certified mail before imposing any fine. TRPA focuses its limited resources on BMP compliance for commercial and large multi-family land uses, which generate the highest pollutant loads.

Real estate professionals are an important resource for BMP education at the point of sale. They help purchasers understand the disclosure requirement and how to submit the form within 30 days. TRPA reports to the Governing Board on how well the enhanced disclosure process is working at each Regional Plan update, when requiring BMP installation at the point of sale is reconsidered.
