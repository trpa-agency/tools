The map shows every parcel in the Tahoe Region colored by BMP status, the area-wide water quality treatment projects, TMDL catchments, and APN labels when you zoom in.

  • Find a parcel by APN or street address, or a catchment by name, and zoom to it.
  • Select parcels by catchment, by area-wide project, or by drawing a rectangle or polygon.
  • Open the selection as a table and download it as CSV (opens in Excel).
  • Click any parcel for its BMP record and a link to print its certificate on Lake Tahoe Info.
  • Turn layers on and off, change the basemap, and save the map as an image or print it.

Replaces the Experience Builder BMP Mapping Tool on gis.trpa.org. Shapefile upload from the old tool is not carried over; contact the BMP Hotline if you need to overlay your own data.
