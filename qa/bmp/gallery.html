Rock types and sizes

What TRPA accepts for each use. Angular rock locks together; rounded rock rolls.

UseSpecification
Infiltration3/4 to 1.5-inch diameter washed, clean drain rock
Cobble6 to 8-inch diameter
Stone, medium9 to 12-inch diameter
Stone, large12-inch diameter and up, depending on purpose and location
Riprap9 to 24-inch diameter, depending on purpose and location
Parking barriersBoulders larger than 3 feet in diameter, set one-third to one-half of their diameter below finished grade. Post bollards 2 feet tall with 12 inches below ground.
Slopes and basinsAngular rock only
Pea gravelNot permitted under roof driplines, in infiltration BMPs, or on sloped areas
Lava rockNot permitted
