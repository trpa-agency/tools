What the status means

BMP CertificateA BMP Certificate of Completion is on file. It stays active as long as the BMPs are maintained and working.
Source Control CertificateSite constraints limit on-site infiltration. The property is in substantial compliance through source control BMPs until an area-wide treatment is available.
Evaluation on fileTRPA has evaluated the property and prescribed BMPs. Install them and call for a final inspection.
No certificateNo BMP record on file. Start with a Small BMP Retrofit Plan or a BMP Retrofit Permit.
Site constraintHigh groundwater, bedrock, slow soils, or utilities limit infiltration on this parcel.
Area-wide treatmentThe parcel drains to a public area-wide water quality treatment project.
RevokedThe certificate has been revoked. Contact the BMP Hotline.

Data: TRPA BMP Database, published through the BMP_Status map service. Searches by parcel number, address, owner name, or certificate number. Assessor's parcel numbers look like 123-456-78 (Washoe, Carson City), 123-456-789 (El Dorado, Placer), or 1234-56-789-012 (Douglas). To print a certificate, open the parcel in the LT Info Parcel Tracker from the detail panel. For a property not shown here or a record that looks wrong, call the BMP Hotline at (775) 589-5202 or email bmp@trpa.gov.
