Small BMP Retrofit Plan

Get a residential BMP plan approved electronically. No trips to the TRPA office.

Homeowners, contractors, and consultants can submit plans for small BMP retrofit proposals that disturb less than 10 cubic yards of soil and get an approval online. You may also pave up to 400 square feet of existing compacted dirt driveway with a Small BMP Retrofit Plan.

Complete the three items below and submit them through Accela Citizen Access, TRPA's online permit portal.

Submit through Accela Citizen Access

The three required items

1

Site plan

A drawing that shows:

  • Parcel boundaries
  • Map scale (if applicable) and north arrow
  • Assessor's parcel number (APN) and property address
  • Location and label for every BMP to be installed
  • Existing coverage (building footprint, driveway, walkways) and stormwater flow lines
  • Stream environment zones (SEZ), if present
2

BMP Treatments Form

Lists the practices you will install and where.

3

Calculation spreadsheet

Sizes each infiltration system for the 20-year, one-hour storm.

Standard conditions

  • Approval of the type, sizing, and location of BMPs is based on the reviewer's assumption that the applicant has accurately and completely measured the impervious areas contributing to each infiltration system.
  • All areas showing erosion or bare soil must be noted on the site plan and revegetated or otherwise stabilized. Vehicle access must be limited to paved areas for a parcel to be eligible for a BMP Certificate of Completion. Parking barriers may be needed to meet this requirement.
  • Small BMP Retrofit Plans are not a verification of land coverage, land capability, or use, and they are not conceptual approval of any future project. Land coverage cannot be verified, transferred, or banked through a Small BMP Retrofit Plan. Those verifications require a separate application to TRPA.

When a Small BMP Retrofit Plan is not enough

If any of the following apply, the project is not eligible for Small BMP Retrofit Plan approval and will need a BMP Retrofit Permit or another TRPA application:

  • Engineered structures, such as retaining walls over 3 feet
  • Extensive grade alterations
  • Infiltration systems capturing runoff from areas larger than 2,500 square feet
  • Excavation deeper than 5 feet
  • Paving more than 400 square feet of existing compacted dirt driveway
  • Subsurface conveyance systems (piping) and drop inlets
  • More than 10 cubic yards of grading
